Understanding Insulin Resistance in PCOS/PMOS

🍬 What is Insulin Resistance?

  • Insulin is a hormone that helps your body use sugar for energy.
  • In insulin resistance, cells don’t respond well to insulin, so the body produces more to compensate.
  • This can lead to higher blood sugar levels and weight gain.

🌸 Link to PCOS/PMOS:

  • Many people with PCOS/PMOS experience insulin resistance, even if they are not overweight.
  • Excess insulin can trigger the ovaries to produce more androgens (male hormones), causing irregular periods, acne, and unwanted hair growth.

⚖️ Health Implications:

  • Increased risk of type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, and heart disease.
  • Can make weight management and fertility more challenging.

🥗 Managing Insulin Resistance:

  • Balanced diet: focus on whole grains, lean proteins, vegetables, and healthy fats.
  • Regular exercise: helps cells use insulin more effectively.
  • Medical support: doctors may recommend medications like metformin to improve insulin sensitivity.

💡 Lifestyle Tips:

  • Avoid excessive sugar and refined carbs.
  • Maintain consistent sleep and stress management.
  • Monitor blood sugar levels if advised by your healthcare provider.
✨

Understanding insulin resistance is key to managing PCOS/PMOS and improving overall health. Small daily changes can make a big difference.
